About Us

Main Project Goal: To develop an AI-driven holistic fitness management system that integrates personalized diet planning, intelligent exercise recommendations, real-time posture detection, and smart nutrition tracking into a unified, user-friendly platform. The objective is to empower individuals with data-driven health insights and accessible AI tools for sustainable lifestyle improvement.

Use Cases

  • Working professionals seeking structured meal planning without time-consuming research.
  • Fitness enthusiasts optimizing body composition through macro and calorie tracking.
  • Individuals correcting posture issues caused by prolonged sitting or improper workout form.
  • Beginners requiring guided exercise recommendations with proper execution details.
  • Home workout users leveraging AI feedback without access to a personal trainer.

Advantages

  • AI-driven personalization instead of generic one-size-fits-all fitness plans.
  • Integrated system combining diet, exercise, posture, and nutrition tracking.
  • Real-time posture feedback using advanced computer vision algorithms.
  • Data-backed insights for measurable progress tracking.
  • Scalable platform suitable for beginners to advanced fitness users.

Features

  • Diet Recommendation: AI-generated weekly meal plans based on calorie calculation and structured food datasets.
  • Exercise Guide: Muscle-specific workout suggestions categorized by difficulty level and equipment type.
  • Posture Detection: Live body landmark detection for exercise form correction using MediaPipe.
  • Nutrition Tracker: Instant macronutrient and calorie analysis with visual representation.
  • AI Assistant: Conversational fitness guidance powered by intelligent response generation.
